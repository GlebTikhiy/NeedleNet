## Play together

1. Everyone installs the same mod version.
2. Launch through Steam → **Multiplayer** → create a session and invite friends.
3. Choose **New game** or **Load game**. The host saves the world.

Share the world, money and progress; keep personal tools. **Y** opens chat; **F2** opens the session panel. Includes the base mod’s extra machines and upgrades.

## Preview status

Passed a 90-minute four-client test with save/load cycles and host-crash recovery. A player also confirmed Steam invitations and co-op play.

Full production chains and purchase progression remain unverified; automated networking tests are local. Some tutorial hints follow shared progress. [Test results](https://github.com/GlebTikhiy/NeedleNet/blob/main/docs/LONG-TEST-RESULTS.md).
